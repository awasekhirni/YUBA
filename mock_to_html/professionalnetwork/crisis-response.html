◆ Response Protocol · Threatening Communication
1
Ensure your physical safety
If you feel physically unsafe, leave the location, call emergency services (10111 for SAPS), and do not engage with the person.
2
Preserve evidence
Screenshot all communication. Do not delete anything. YUBA auto-exports and time-stamps all messages for legal use.
3
Report on YUBA
File the incident report. Auto-assigns a safety specialist within 15 minutes. Full audit trail created.
4
Notify workplace
Report to HR and, if applicable, your union representative. YUBA can auto-notify both.
5
Legal escalation (if needed)
For threats of violence, YUBA will help you file with SAPS and obtain a protection order. Legal aid available.
6
Ongoing monitoring
YUBA monitors for 90 days post-incident. Any new contact auto-alerts your safety specialist.
