1. Opening Hook

Last week the CCMA issued a ruling that fundamentally alters how employers must handle remote work accommodations. Here's what it means for you, in 3 minutes.

2. Main Article · Full Analysis

The commissioner held that failure to provide reasonable accommodations for home office setup may constitute unfair labor practice under section 186(1)(b) of the LRA.

3 actions to take this week:

→ Review your remote work policy for gaps

→ Document your current accommodations

→ Brief your HR team on the new precedent
