Job Description

We are seeking an experienced Senior Labor Relations Manager to lead our industrial relations strategy across 12 business units in Southern Africa. You will be responsible for negotiating collective agreements, managing dispute resolution, and ensuring compliance with labor legislation.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead collective bargaining negotiations with recognized unions
  • Manage CCMA and Labour Court disputes end-to-end
  • Develop and implement labor relations policies
  • Advise executive leadership on IR risk and strategy
  • Mentor junior IR specialists across the group

Requirements

  • LLB or LLM in Labor Law (or related)
  • 12+ years of progressive labor relations experience
  • Proven track record in collective bargaining
  • Admitted attorney with CCMA accreditation preferred
