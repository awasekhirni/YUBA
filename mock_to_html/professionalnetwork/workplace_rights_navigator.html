Featured Guide · Unfair Dismissal · 6-Step Process
1
Confirm Your Rights

Under LRA, you have 30 days to refer a dismissal dispute to CCMA. Confirm your dismissal type and eligibility.

2
Gather Evidence

Employment contract, dismissal letter, performance records, witness statements, and relevant communication.

3
Request Reasons

Submit written request for dismissal reasons. Employer must respond in writing within a reasonable period.

4
Refer to CCMA

File Form LRA 7.1 with CCMA within 30 days. YUBA provides auto-generated forms and pre-filled templates.

5
Conciliation

Attend conciliation within 30 days of referral. Bring all evidence. YUBA provides a coach for preparation.

6
Arbitration

If unresolved, matter proceeds to arbitration. You may represent yourself or appoint a representative.
