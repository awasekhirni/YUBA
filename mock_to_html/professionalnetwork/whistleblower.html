◆ Your Protection
✓ Protected Disclosures Act (PDA)

South African law protects employees who report misconduct from retaliation, dismissal, or any occupational detriment.

✓ POPIA Compliance

You have the right to report misconduct. Your identity is a protected data subject right under POPIA.

✓ End-to-End Encryption

Reports are encrypted with AES-256 before leaving your device. The encryption key is known only to you and the recipient.

✓ Zero-Knowledge Architecture

YUBA servers hold only encrypted blobs. We literally cannot read your report even if compelled by court order.

✓ Legal Support

If you face retaliation, YUBA provides free legal support. We've successfully defended 42 whistleblowers.
