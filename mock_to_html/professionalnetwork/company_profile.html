About Sasol

Sasol is an integrated energy and chemical company with a diversified portfolio of businesses spanning mining, exploration, production, refining, and chemicals. With operations in over 30 countries and a workforce of 12,400+, Sasol is one of South Africa's largest employers and a key player in the global energy transition.
