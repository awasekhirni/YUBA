⚖ Conflict Resolution Used 8 times
The Sasol shift dispute that became a partnership
SituationIn 2022, 400+ Sasol workers threatened strike over proposed shift changes that would affect weekend schedules.
TaskAs Senior LR Specialist, I had to prevent the strike while balancing operational needs and worker wellbeing.
ActionI designed a 3-phase consultation with union leadership, including data transparency on production needs and worker surveys on preferences. Facilitated 6 workshops to co-design an alternative.
ResultStrike averted. Alternative shift model adopted. Retention improved 12%. Model later rolled out to 3 other business units.
👥 Leadership Used 6 times
Influencing executive leadership without authority
SituationOur IR team identified a compliance risk in a new policy. Leadership had already approved it.
TaskAs the newest senior hire, I needed to challenge a decision made by senior leadership.
ActionRather than push back publicly, I prepared a 1-page brief with 3 risk scenarios and mitigation options. Requested a 1:1 with the CHRO. Framed it as "helping de-risk the decision," not "you're wrong."
ResultPolicy was paused and reworked. I was asked to lead the redesign. Now the CHRO consults me on major IR decisions before approval.
🎯 Strategic Thinking Used 4 times
Designing a 3-year IR strategy for 4-country operation
SituationSasol's expansion into 4 new SADC markets exposed us to varied labor laws and union structures.
TaskBuild a unified IR framework that respected local realities.
ActionLed a 6-month research project, interviewed 42 stakeholders, benchmarked 12 peer multinationals, and designed a modular framework with core standards plus local flexibility.
ResultAdopted by leadership. Reduced cross-border disputes by 62% in first 18 months. Framework adopted by 2 peer companies.
📚 Failure & Learning Used 3 times
The policy rollout I rushed and had to reverse
SituationI pushed a workplace flexibility policy through in 2 weeks to "move fast."
TaskWanted to prove value quickly as a new senior hire.
ActionSkipped middle management consultation. Rolled it out. Within a month, managers were pushing back hard. I had to reverse it publicly.
ResultHumiliating, but a pivotal lesson. I now involve stakeholders 2 levels down from day one. The revised policy, launched 3 months later, is now a case study for how to do change properly.
💡 Innovation Used 5 times

SituationOur CCMA dispute intake was entirely manual and error-prone.
TaskReduce handling time and improve compliance accuracy.
ActionMapped the workflow, designed a digital intake with auto-classification, and piloted it with 3 CCMA cases.
Result40 hours/month saved. 100% CCMA compliance on filings. Now standard practice across all Sasol IR teams.
🌟 Mentorship Used 4 times
Coaching a junior through her first complex case
SituationA junior IR officer was assigned her first complex disciplinary matter.
TaskGuide her without taking over the case.
ActionSet up weekly 1:1s, let her lead every hearing while I observed, and used the "what would you do next?" coaching method instead of giving answers.
ResultShe won the case. She's since been promoted and now mentors 3 juniors herself. The coaching method is now our team standard.
